WHOOP gets better every week. This page is a running record of what we’ve shipped — new features, smarter algorithms, and improvements that make your data more accurate and your experience smoother. Check back regularly.

## **August 2026**

### **Explore the Galleri® multi-cancer early detection test**

_July 30, 2026_

WHOOP Advanced Labs now offers the Galleri® test by GRAIL, which screens for a signal associated with more than 50 types of cancer, including cancers with no recommended routine screening. It can detect that signal before symptoms appear, when cancer may be more treatable.

### **WHOOP now connects with Natural Cycles**

_August 4th, 2026_

Connect WHOOP to Natural Cycles for personalized insights across your reproductive health journey — from hormone-free birth control and pregnancy planning to pregnancy and perimenopause. Natural Cycles uses overnight skin-temperature data from compatible WHOOP devices as part of its algorithm to deliver insights in the Natural Cycles app. Eligible WHOOP members who are new to Natural Cycles can get their first year free.

### **WHOOP Advanced Labs is now available without a wearable**

_August 18th, 2026_

WHOOP Advanced Labs is now available to people without a WHOOP wearable. That means more people can access comprehensive or specialized blood testing, clinician-reviewed results, and personalized next steps to better understand what’s happening beneath the surface.

## **July 2026**

### **Ask WHOOP for a chart of your trends**

_July 29_

WHOOP AI can now turn trend questions into charts built for what you ask. Ask about how your data has changed over time, or how two metrics move together, and WHOOP shows the chart alongside a plain-language read on what it means.

Try a follow-up like “make it 90 days” or “group it by week” to refine the view in the conversation.

Access in WHOOP AI.

### **Log your Journal by voice or text**

_July 29_

You can now interact with WHOOP AI directly from the Journal page using voice or text. Mention behaviors in the conversation and WHOOP logs them for you; ask to add or remove a behavior and WHOOP guides you through the update before saving it.

Open the Journal manually to use voice or text entry. This is not available from the daily Journal pop-up.

### **More accurate heart rate where it’s hardest to measure**

_July 6_

WHOOP has a new heart rate algorithm that improves accuracy across activities, with the biggest gains in weightlifting, functional fitness, HIIT, cycling, and golf. Because Strain is calculated from heart rate, some members may notice their Strain shift as the underlying heart rate data gets more accurate.

This happens automatically. No action needed.

[A Look Behind The Data: How WHOOP Measures Heart Rate](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/a-look-behind-the-data-how-whoop-measures-heart-rate/)

[WHOOP Strain Explained: How Your Effort Is Measured](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/how-does-whoop-strain-work-101/)

### **Fine-tune your activity timing with your heart rate**

_July 1_

Editing an activity is now more precise on iOS. A heart rate graph is built directly into the Edit Activity screen, so you can scrub through your data to adjust exactly when an activity started and ended.

Whether you're trimming a warm-up, cutting a cool-down, or correcting an automatically detected workout, your heart rate now provides a clearer guide for setting the right time bounds.

Edit from the Activity Details page by tapping the edit icon.

## **June 2026**

### **Manage your activities right from chat**

_June 29_

Add, edit, or delete an activity just by asking WHOOP in chat. If you logged the wrong workout or forgot to start one, tell WHOOP and it makes the fix — no menus required.

Just ask in any WHOOP AI conversation.

### **A new way to edit when your activity started and ended**

_June 25_ On the Edit Activity screen, you can now scrub across your heart rate graph to set the precise start and end of an activity. Trim a warm-up, cut a cool-down, or capture a stretch WHOOP didn’t include.

Edit from the Activity Details page by tapping the edit icon above the heart rate graph.

### **Fragmented activities, merged automatically**

_June 23_

When WHOOP detects a single activity as several broken-up pieces, it now stitches them back together. If two detected activities end and start within 60 minutes of each other, WHOOP merges them into one — so a round of golf, a day on the slopes, or a long hike shows up as one activity instead of a handful.

This happens automatically. No action needed.

[How WHOOP detects and labels your workouts](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/how-whoop-detects-and-labels-your-workouts-activities/)

### **Connect your health records with HealthEx**

_June 17_ Connect your electronic health records securely through [HealthEx](https://www.healthex.io/) and WHOOP can draw on clinical context like conditions, medications, procedures, and labs alongside your daily data. Pairing that history with what WHOOP measures every day gives a more complete picture of your health. Available to US members 18 and older. Nothing imports without your consent, and you can disconnect at any time.

Once connected, WHOOP builds your Health Record Analysis: a snapshot of the health record information now available to your coaching context. The Integration Details screen shows what’s connected, lets you refresh to get the latest records, and gives you the option to disconnect if you choose to stop sharing your data.

Connect in More > App Settings > Integrations.

[How to connect your medical records in the WHOOP app](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/healthex-integration/)

## **May 2026**

### **A deeper integration with Strava**

_May 21_

WHOOP and Strava now work better together. Activities recorded on Strava import into WHOOP automatically. So do activities that other devices route through Strava — a run with another wearable device, an indoor ride from Zwift, a lift logged in Hevy.

Each imported activity can carry:

- GPS, distance, pace/speed for cardio activities (when available).
- Power and cadence when the original device captured them.
- Exercises with sets, reps, and weights when the activity is strength training and the source app logged that detail.
- Source attribution — "Garmin Via Strava" when another device routed through. You can see where the data came from on the activity details screen.
- If an activity overlaps something WHOOP already recorded, WHOOP keeps the version with the richer data. No duplicates.

What flows from WHOOP into Strava

Any activity recorded on WHOOP can flow to Strava. In the Strava integration settings page, you can choose to have all activities shared to Strava, specific activity types shared, or you can share one-off via the Activity Details page. If you enable Strength Trainer workouts you record on WHOOP to flow to Strava, then WHOOP will share the full structure of the session,

In the Strava feed, your strength workouts show up with:

- The list of exercises you performed, with weights
- Total volume lifted
- Muscle groups worked, rendered as a muscle map
- WHOOP attribution as the source

Enable in More > App Settings > Integrations.

[How WHOOP and Strava work together](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/us/en/thelocker/how-whoop-and-strava-work-together/?srsltid=AfmBOorPZuHFVhj4F9TbfPtj9K3T8UPbMobunM8Gc9AECJZ08jg_YTG-)

### **Workout detection and labels, even more accurate**

_May 20_

Workout Auto-Detect and Auto-Classify both got smarter. The latest release reflects a step forward in both the scale of data and the sophistication of the AI models behind workout detection.

Recent improvements focus on areas that have historically been harder to detect and classify:

- More accurate detection of strength training and functional fitness
- Better identification of lower-strain, sustained activity
- Fewer workouts labeled as generic “Activity” due to improved classification confidence
- Increased consistency across age groups and fitness levels

The updated system was trained on a larger, more diverse dataset and a new generation of advanced machine learning models that better understand your workouts.

[How WHOOP detects and labels your workouts](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/us/en/thelocker/how-whoop-detects-and-labels-your-workouts-activities/?srsltid=AfmBOoodLUPB0wYV4JsEy8NhI2vpFRJa41U6VC8Dh5z3gZo1PCu81MxH)

### **Coaching that remembers**

_May 1_

WHOOP learns what matters to you, from your goals to your routines, to how you like to be coached. WHOOP uses that to give you guidance that applies to your life. My Memory is where all of that lives. You can see what WHOOP knows about your life, add details to make your coaching more relevant, or delete anything you don't want stored.

My Memory shows the personal context behind your guidance, organized across categories:

- Goals: what you're working toward
- Identity: what makes you you – what you do day to day, your family, location
- Lifestyle: your routines and habits
- Preferences: how you want to be coached
- Events: what's coming up
- Health History: how conditions, injuries, or illnesses impact you
- Mood: how you feel day to day

Everything in My Memory is yours to manage. You can view it, add to it, edit it, or remove it. You can also turn memory off entirely. WHOOP automatically keeps your Memory up-to-date. As your life changes, WHOOP will remember temporary patterns or things about you, like past sickness, but won’t actively coach you on them.

[Learn more about My Memory](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/us/en/thelocker/my-memory-whoop/?srsltid=AfmBOoovnjCVTaXjInZYJozNkiQq2bX6KannCKxfk90Q1fTbwDZ3ugMf)
